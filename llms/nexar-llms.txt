# Nexar (nexar-ai.com)

> Nexar operates one of the largest real-world video driving datasets - 1.2B+
> miles of real driving captured each year by 350,000+ connected dashcams - and
> builds the data, models, and real-time intelligence used to train and validate
> autonomous-vehicle, ADAS, and smart-city systems.

## Products & platforms
- BADAS 2.0 - collision-prediction model (99.4% precision, 91% recall): https://www.nexar-ai.com/badas-2
- Nexar APEX - real-world validation benchmark for AV stacks: https://www.nexar-ai.com/drivers-license-for-av
- Nexar Atlas - real-world driving data, AI-ready at scale: https://www.nexar-ai.com/nexar-atlas
- The City AV Index - U.S. city AV-readiness index: https://www.nexar-ai.com/the-av-city-index
